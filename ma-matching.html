Machine Learning Models
Ensemble Intelligence

Four Models. One Verdict.

Each target company is evaluated by four independent ML models. Their predictions are combined through a meta-learner that outperforms any individual model, creating a robust classification system with 94% AUC on distressed asset identification.

XGBoost Distressed Classifier

Gradient-boosted ensemble trained on 15,000+ historical distressed/healthy company outcomes. Features include 40+ financial ratios, 8-quarter trend features, macroeconomic indicators, and industry-relative metrics. Optuna-tuned with 500 trials achieving 94% AUC. SHAP values provide per-prediction explainability so analysts understand exactly why a company was flagged.

Anomaly Detection (Isolation Forest)

Unsupervised model that identifies financial statement anomalies without labeled data. Detects earnings manipulation, unusual accrual patterns, off-balance-sheet arrangements, and accounting irregularities that traditional ratio analysis misses. Runs on every new filing within minutes of SEC submission, flagging companies that deviate from their industry cluster and historical patterns.

NLP Sentiment & Signal Extractor

Transformer-based NLP model fine-tuned on financial text. Analyzes earnings call transcripts, 10-K management discussion sections, news articles, and analyst reports. Extracts M&A-relevant signals: management tone shifts, competitive pressure language, restructuring hints, and strategic pivot indicators. Sentiment scored on a -1 to +1 scale with confidence intervals.

Meta-Learner (Stacked Ensemble)

A logistic regression meta-learner that combines predictions from XGBoost, Isolation Forest, and NLP models. Trained with cross-validated stacking to prevent overfitting. The meta-learner learns when to trust each base model, weighing XGBoost more heavily for financial ratio signals and NLP more heavily for early-stage strategic shifts that haven't yet manifested in financials.

Unique Differentiator

Where M&A Meets Supply Chain Intelligence

This is not a standalone M&A tool. It is deeply integrated with the supply chain risk platform, creating a unique intelligence feedback loop that no traditional M&A advisory firm can replicate.

Supply Chain → M&A Signal

When the supply chain platform detects a supplier's risk score climbing above 80 for 3 consecutive months, the M&A pipeline automatically adds that supplier to the distressed asset watchlist. The converse is also true: a supplier with improving operational metrics and expanding capacity becomes a strategic acquisition candidate.

This creates an asymmetric information advantage. We know a supplier is deteriorating months before it shows up in their financial statements, because we are already monitoring their on-time delivery rates, quality scores, and compliance status in real time through the supply chain platform.

M&A → Supply Chain Enrichment

When a target is acquired, the M&A pipeline feeds back into the supply chain platform: new entity relationships are mapped, risk profiles are recalibrated, compliance requirements are updated across all 6 regulatory frameworks, and demand forecasts incorporate the expanded capacity and new geographic reach.

Post-acquisition integration monitoring tracks whether the anticipated synergies materialize. If the acquired entity's operational metrics don't converge toward the acquirer's baseline within 12 months, automated alerts trigger a strategic review. This closes the loop from identification to integration validation.
